\section{Conclusion}
We address machine unlearning for privacy-critical deep embedding-based biometric systems, spanning fingerprint, iris, and face recognition, where existing many-to-one collapse causes prolonged convergence under frequent edge-device unlearning requests. Our Camouflage Unlearning achieves up to 4$\times$ speedup via many-to-many distribution and surgical parameter modifications, with forgetting, retention, and privacy comparable to state-of-the-art methods. Future work extends CU to continual forgetting in pretrained biometric models.
